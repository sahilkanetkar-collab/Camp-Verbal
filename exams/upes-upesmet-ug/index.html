Overview

UPESMET-UG (UPES Dehradun) is run by UPES, Dehradun for admission to IPM, BBA, BCom, Finance and Liberal Arts programmes. 2 colleges listed here accept it, including UPES School of Business and UPES School of Liberal Studies and Media.

UPESMET-UG is only for BBA and Integrated BBA-MBA. Students with 60% (BBA) or 75% (BBA-MBA) in both X and XII can skip the test through the Board Merit route; UGAT and CUET-UG scores also get an exemption. B.Com and BA admit on interview or CUET. UPES does not offer BA Economics. UPES has run the test in several phases through the year, so a missed phase is not fatal.

How selection works

UPESMET-UG score (or a valid UGAT / CUET-UG score, or Board Merit) → Personal Interview → offer. B.Com (Hons) and BA (Hons) do not use UPESMET-UG: they admit on PI / CUET.
