Overview

JSAT (Jindal) is run by O.P. Jindal Global University (delivered by Pearson VUE) for admission to IPM, BBA, BCom, Economics, Finance and Liberal Arts programmes. 1 college accepts it: O.P. Jindal Global University (JGBS, JSBF, JSGP, JSLH, JSIA and other schools).

LSAT is no longer used for JGU's non-law UG programmes (law now uses LNAT-UK). JSAT can be taken from home; apply early because seats fill first-come.

Common questions

When is JSAT (Jindal) 2027?

Official 2027 dates are not out yet. Non-law UG 2027 notice awaited as of Oct 2026. 2026 cycle was rolling ~Oct 2025–Jul 2026 with on-demand JSAT slots. Last year's exam was held on On-demand slots chosen by candidate.

Who can apply for JSAT (Jindal)?

Class XII from a recognised board (any stream; Maths preferred for BBA Business Analytics/Financial Markets). Some BA schools cite ~45–50% minimum — check per school.

What is the JSAT (Jindal) exam pattern?

120 questions, 120 minutes. English Verbal Ability (40 questions); Logical Reasoning (40 questions); Quantitative Skills (40 questions). Marking: No negative marking

Which colleges accept JSAT (Jindal)?

O.P. Jindal Global University (JGBS, JSBF, JSGP, JSLH, JSIA and other schools).

How much English is in JSAT (Jindal)?

How does selection work after JSAT (Jindal)?

Application (+ personal statement; 1,000-word statement for Liberal Arts) → JSAT ≥50% OR SAT 1100+/ACT 27+/CUET-UG or UGAT 60 percentile+ → online faculty interview → offer based on X/XII marks, test, statement and interview.
