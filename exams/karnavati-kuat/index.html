Overview

Karnavati KUAT is run by Karnavati University, Gandhinagar for admission to BBA, BCom, Finance and Liberal Arts programmes. 2 colleges listed here accept it, including Unitedworld School of Business, Karnavati University and Unitedworld School of Liberal Arts & Mass Communication, Karnavati University.

Karnavati's official site confirms KUAT and open 2026-27 applications but gives few details. Most facts here are from Careers360; confirm by phone (9667048555). Karnavati is also a participating institute in AIMA UGAT.
