Overview

BUMAT is run by Bharati Vidyapeeth (Deemed to be University), Pune for admission to BBA programmes. 4 colleges listed here accept it, including IMED, Pune (Institute of Management & Entrepreneurship Development), BVIMR, New Delhi (Institute of Management & Research) and BVDMS, Navi Mumbai (Dept of Management Studies).

Official name on BVDU's 2026 schedule is BUMAT-2026 (BBA/BCA). Apply at bvducet.bharatividyapeeth.edu. BVDU also runs a separate BV-BCom test for B.Com/B.Com (Hons) on the same June dates. No donation/capitation fee. 2027 forms are expected around Nov 2026 (based on 2026; not official).

Common questions

When is BUMAT 2027?

Official 2027 dates are not out yet. 2026 forms opened Nov 2025, closed 5 Jun; test 13 Jun. Expect 2027 forms from Nov 2026 and a mid-June 2027 test. Last year's exam was held on 13 Jun 2026.

Who can apply for BUMAT?

Passed Class XII (HSC) or equivalent from a recognised board; appearing students can apply. Indian nationals only. The official 2026 brochure sets no minimum % (coaching sites mention 45%). No age limit.

What is the BUMAT exam pattern?

200 questions, 150 minutes. Numerical Aptitude (60 questions); Reasoning / Logical Aptitude (60 questions); Verbal Ability (60 questions); Reading Comprehension (20 questions). Marking: +1 per question (200 marks); no negative marking

Which colleges accept BUMAT?

IMED, Pune (Institute of Management & Entrepreneurship Development), BVIMR, New Delhi (Institute of Management & Research), BVDMS, Navi Mumbai (Dept of Management Studies), BVDU smaller campuses: IM Kolhapur, YMIM Karad, IMRDA Sangli, AKIMSS Solapur.

How much English is in BUMAT?

80 of 200 questions are Verbal Ability (60) + Reading Comprehension (20)

How does selection work after BUMAT?

Merit list on 200-mark test → counselling to BVU management institutes
