Common questions

Which entrance exams does O.P. Jindal Global University accept?

O.P. Jindal Global University accepts JSAT (Jindal) and SAT (Digital SAT) for the programmes listed in this guide.

What is the cutoff for O.P. Jindal Global University?

JSAT (Jindal): Qualifying: JSAT 50% overall; or SAT 1100 / ACT 27 / CUET-UG 60 percentile (unofficial, 2026); SAT (Digital SAT): SAT 1100+ (in lieu of JSAT) (unofficial, 2026).

Which UG programmes does O.P. Jindal Global University offer?

BBA (Hons) — Marketing/HR/Finance, Business Analytics, Financial Markets, Family Business; Integrated BBA (Hons) + MBA; BCom (Hons) — with ACCA, Capital Markets, Finance & Entrepreneurship; BA/BSc (Hons) Economics; BA (Hons) PPE; BA (Hons) Liberal Arts & Humanities; BA (Hons) Global Affairs, Political Science, Psychology, English, Journalism etc.; BBA, BCom, BA/BSc Economics, BA Liberal Arts, PPE.
