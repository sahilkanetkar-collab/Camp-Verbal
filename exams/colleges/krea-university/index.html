Common questions

Which entrance exams does Krea University accept?

Krea University accepts IPMAT Indore, Krea KAT and SAT (Digital SAT) for the programmes listed in this guide.

What is the cutoff for Krea University?

Krea KAT: Minimum test requirement: KAT ≥15 in each of Problem Solving, Critical Thinking and Numerical Reasoning; or SAT 1300 with Math 600; or IPMAT 2025/2026 at or above IIM Indore EWS cutoff; or JEE Main at or above IIT EWS cutoff (official, 2026); SAT (Digital SAT): Integrated MBA: SAT 1300 overall with Math 600 minimum; SIAS: 'eligible score' not specified (official, 2026).

Which UG programmes does Krea University offer?

Integrated management programme; BA / BA (Hons) / BSc / BSc (Hons) — majors incl. Economics, Business Studies & Finance, Politics, Psychology, Literature, History, CS, Maths etc.; 5-yr Integrated MBA (BBA/BBA Hons + MBA); exit after 3 or 4 yrs with BBA; BA/BSc (Hons); Integrated MBA.
