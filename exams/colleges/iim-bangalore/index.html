Common questions

Which entrance exams does IIM Bangalore accept?

IIM Bangalore accepts IIMB DBE Entrance Test and IIMB UG Admissions Test for the programmes listed in this guide.

What is the cutoff for IIM Bangalore?

IIMB DBE Entrance Test: Previous-cycle Gen shortlist cutoff: 50% (IIMB test) / 70th percentile (CUET) / 50th percentile (JEE Main); NC-OBC & EWS 45%/65/45; SC/ST/PwD 40%/60/40 (official, 2025).

Which UG programmes does IIM Bangalore offer?

BBA in Digital Business & Entrepreneurship — 3-yr online (up to 6 yrs), exams at centres; BSc (Hons) Economics (minors: Data Science, Business Studies) — 4-yr; BSc (Hons) Data Science (minors: Economics, Business Studies) — 4-yr.

Where is IIM Bangalore?

Online, Karnataka.
