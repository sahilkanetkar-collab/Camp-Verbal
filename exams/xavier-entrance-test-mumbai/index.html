Overview

XET (St. Xavier's Mumbai) is run by St. Xavier's College, Mumbai for admission to BMS and BCom programmes. 1 college accepts it: St. Xavier's College (Autonomous).

Xavier's runs its own test, not MAH CET CAP. XET also covers B.Sc Data Science & AI, B.Sc IT and BA Mass Communication. BA Economics at Xavier's is not on the XET list for 2026.

Dates 2027 admissions

Official 2027 dates are not out yet. 2026 applications opened early Feb; XET 2–8 May. Expect applications Feb 2027, test early May
