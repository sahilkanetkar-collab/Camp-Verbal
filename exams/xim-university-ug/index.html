Overview

XIM University UG (merit + PI) is run by XIM University, Bhubaneswar for admission to BBA, BCom, Economics and Finance programmes. 1 college accepts it: XIM University (School of Commerce / School of Economics).

XIM University (formerly Xavier University, Bhubaneswar) is the parent of XIMB; its UG campus is at Harirajpur, Puri district, about 25 km from Bhubaneswar. There is no entrance test for BBM / B.Com / BSc Economics — good marks in XII plus the interview are what count. The programme page describes a 3-year, 6-semester course while the fee table lists four years; confirm the duration with admissions (0674-6647766).

How selection works

Online application at xim.edu.in → merit on Class X and XII marks → personal interview → offer. 85%+ in XII earns a 50% tuition waiver (kept if CGPA ≥ 8.0 each year).
