Overview

NMIMS NPAT is run by SVKM's NMIMS (Deemed-to-be University); test delivered by Pearson VUE for admission to IPM, BBA, BCom, Economics, Finance and Liberal Arts programmes. 12 colleges listed here accept it, including NMIMS Anil Surendra Modi School of Commerce (ASMSOC), NMIMS Sarla Anil Modi School of Economics (SAMSOE) and NMIMS Jyoti Dalal School of Liberal Arts.

Up to 5 programmes per application; no management quota. Fees shown are the latest Careers360 figures, not official 2026-27 or 2027-28 fees. NPAT is used only by NMIMS. Dhule/Shirpur campuses appear in some coaching lists but are not among the 7 campuses on the official NPAT page.

How selection works

Merit list on NPAT score only (no interview/WAT). Common registration with campus preferences; Phase 1 admits can upgrade campus in Phase 2 by merit number and seat availability.

Common questions

When is NMIMS NPAT 2027?

Official 2027 dates are not out yet. Registration usually opens late Jan; tests in slots from Feb to early Jun (2026: opened 28 Jan). Last year's exam was held on Phase 1: 10 Feb – 31 Mar 2026; Phase 2: 1 Apr – 1 Jun 2026 (candidate picks slot).

Who can apply for NMIMS NPAT?

Class XII passed/appearing; 50% aggregate for most programmes (60% reported for BBA International Business and BSc Economics Mumbai); Maths/Stats in XII needed for BBA, BCom, BSc Finance, BSc Economics, BBA FinTech, BBA Finance, Integrated BBA-MBA; age ≤ 25. Check school-wise rules in the NPAT handbook.

What is the NMIMS NPAT exam pattern?

120 questions, 100 minutes. Quantitative & Numerical Ability (40 questions); Reasoning & General Intelligence (40 questions); Verbal Reasoning (English) (40 questions). Marking: +1 per correct; no negative marking

Which colleges accept NMIMS NPAT?

NMIMS Anil Surendra Modi School of Commerce (ASMSOC), NMIMS Sarla Anil Modi School of Economics (SAMSOE), NMIMS Jyoti Dalal School of Liberal Arts, NMIMS Pravin Dalal School of Entrepreneurship & Family Business Management, NMIMS School of Branding & Advertising, NMIMS Centre for International Studies, NMIMS Navi Mumbai campus, NMIMS Bengaluru campus, NMIMS Hyderabad campus, NMIMS Indore campus, NMIMS Chandigarh campus, NMIMS Ahmedabad campus.

How much English is in NMIMS NPAT?

40 of 120 questions (one-third) are Verbal Reasoning

How does selection work after NMIMS NPAT?

Merit list on NPAT score only (no interview/WAT). Common registration with campus preferences; Phase 1 admits can upgrade campus in Phase 2 by merit number and seat availability.
