Overview

SAT (Digital SAT) is run by College Board (USA) for admission to IPM, BBA, BMS, BCom, Economics, Finance and Liberal Arts programmes. 12 colleges listed here accept it, including Ashoka University, FLAME University and Krea University (SIAS & IFMR GSB Integrated MBA).

College Board's India list also includes Plaksha, SRM AP, Amity and Alliance (covered elsewhere or outside this site's scope). Woxsen also accepts IPMAT for BBA. The pattern shown is the standard Digital SAT format, the same worldwide. Remember to send scores to each university using its DI code.

How selection works

SAT score is sent to the university and used in place of (or alongside) its own test; each university then runs its own essay/interview stage.

Common questions

When is SAT (Digital SAT) 2027?

The 2027 exam is scheduled for 7 Nov 2026, 5 Dec 2026, 6 Mar 2027, 1 May 2027, 5 Jun 2027. Last year's exam was held on Multiple international dates per year.

Who can apply for SAT (Digital SAT)?

No formal eligibility; usually Class XI–XII students.

What is the SAT (Digital SAT) exam pattern?

98 questions, 134 minutes. Reading and Writing (54 questions); Math (44 questions). Marking: Scaled 400–1600 (200–800 per section); no negative marking

Which colleges accept SAT (Digital SAT)?

Ashoka University, FLAME University, Krea University (SIAS & IFMR GSB Integrated MBA), O.P. Jindal Global University, Shiv Nadar University, Delhi-NCR, Azim Premji University, Ahmedabad University, Mahindra University, Woxsen University, Sai University, NMIMS (Deemed University), Bennett University.

How much English is in SAT (Digital SAT)?

54 of 98 questions are Reading & Writing (half the scaled score)

How does selection work after SAT (Digital SAT)?

SAT score is sent to the university and used in place of (or alongside) its own test; each university then runs its own essay/interview stage.
