Overview

AIMA UGAT is run by All India Management Association (AIMA) for admission to IPM, BBA, BCom and Finance programmes. 6 colleges listed here accept it, including KIIT School of Management, Alliance University and GITAM (Deemed to be) University.

Low-stakes test mostly used by private universities. Ahmedabad University (Amrut Mody School) is listed as a participating institute in the 2026 prospectus. AIMA warns students to verify each institute's recognition themselves.

Dates 2027 admissions

Official 2027 dates are not out yet. 2026 applications until 6 Jun; paper-based test 14 Jun. Expect a May–Jun 2027 test

Common questions

When is AIMA UGAT 2027?

Official 2027 dates are not out yet. 2026 applications until 6 Jun; paper-based test 14 Jun. Expect a May–Jun 2027 test. Last year's exam was held on 14 Jun 2026 (PBT).

Who can apply for AIMA UGAT?

Set by each participating institute (typically Class XII passed/appearing)

What is the AIMA UGAT exam pattern?

130 questions, 120 minutes. English Language (40 questions); Numerical & Data Analysis (30 questions); Reasoning & Intelligence (30 questions); General Knowledge (30 questions). Marking: +1 per question; negative marking applies (amount not stated in bulletin)

Which colleges accept AIMA UGAT?

KIIT School of Management, Alliance University, GITAM (Deemed to be) University, Unitedworld School of Business, Karnavati University, Jagannath Institute of Management Sciences / New Delhi Institute of Management, NICMAR University; ITM Skills University; Sharda University; Birla Global Univ; ASBM Univ; ISBR; ISME; and others.

How much English is in AIMA UGAT?

40 of 130 questions are English Language

How does selection work after AIMA UGAT?

UGAT score → each institute's own shortlisting/GD/PI/counselling
