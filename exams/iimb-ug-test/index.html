Overview

IIMB UG Admissions Test is run by IIM Bangalore (Dorabji Tata School of Undergraduate Studies) for admission to Economics programmes. 1 college accepts it: IIM Bangalore – Dorabji Tata School of Undergraduate Studies.

Different calendar from other IIM UG tests: the test is held in Nov–Dec of Class XII, not in May. The 2027-intake test is on 15 Nov 2026 and applications have already closed.

Common questions

When is IIMB UG Admissions Test 2027?

The 2027 exam is scheduled for 15 Nov 2026. Last year's exam was held on 13 Dec 2025.

Who can apply for IIMB UG Admissions Test?

Class XII with Mathematics in XI and XII; at least 60% in Class X Mathematics; age ≤ 20 on 1 Aug of the admission year.

What is the IIMB UG Admissions Test exam pattern?

60 questions, 120 minutes. English Comprehension (15 questions); Quantitative & Data Interpretation (30 questions); Logical Reasoning (15 questions). Marking: MCQ; 1/3 of the question's marks deducted for a wrong answer (reported as +3 / −1)

Which colleges accept IIMB UG Admissions Test?

IIM Bangalore – Dorabji Tata School of Undergraduate Studies.

How much English is in IIMB UG Admissions Test?

15 of 60 questions are English Comprehension

How does selection work after IIMB UG Admissions Test?

Test score → interview shortlist → final composite: test 60% + personal interview 40% (IIMB FAQ). Careers360 reports a more detailed split that also uses Class X marks; this is not official.
