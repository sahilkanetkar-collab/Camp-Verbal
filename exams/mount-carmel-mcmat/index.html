Overview

Mount Carmel (interview) is run by Mount Carmel College (Autonomous), Bengaluru for admission to BBA, BCom and Finance programmes. 1 college accepts it: Mount Carmel College (Autonomous).

Name 'MCMAT' kept for search, but the official 2026-27 prospectus shows interview-based selection with no entrance test. Women-only at UG level.

Exam pattern

The section-wise pattern is not published.

Marking: Not applicable The official 2026-27 prospectus lists a Personal Interview for BBA and does not mention MCMAT. Older aggregator pages describe an MCMAT test (reasoning, maths, English); treat that as outdated unless the college announces it.

English & verbal: None (no written test); English matters in the interview

How selection works

Online application → shortlisting → Personal Interview. Programme fee is communicated after shortlisting. 50% of intake reserved for the Roman Catholic community.
