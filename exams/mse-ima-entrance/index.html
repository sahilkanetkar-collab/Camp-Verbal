Overview

MSE Integrated MA Economics Test is run by Madras School of Economics, Chennai (applications handled via IBPS) for admission to Economics programmes. 1 college accepts it: Madras School of Economics (MSE).

One of the few serious economics-only institutes you can join straight after Class XII, at fees far below private universities. Maths in XII is compulsory and the XII exam must be cleared in the first attempt. The integrated MA is new (first batches only recently graduated), so there is no placement record yet; MSE's reputation rests on its MA/PGDM placements. Hostels exist on campus; charges not published.

How selection works

Entrance test → merit list (ties broken by XII percentage, then age) → counselling → document verification and orientation at MSE. 35% of seats are reserved for Tamil Nadu residents; central reservation also applies.
