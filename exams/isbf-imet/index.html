Overview

ISBF iMET (Delhi) is run by Indian School of Business & Finance (ISBF), New Delhi for admission to BBA, Economics and Finance programmes. 1 college accepts it: Indian School of Business & Finance (ISBF).

The degree is awarded by the University of London (academic direction from LSE), not by an Indian university, so it sits outside UGC; ISBF suggests enrolling in parallel with Delhi University's School of Open Learning if you want a UGC-recognised degree too. Top performers can transfer to LSE in year 2. Scholarships of up to 50% of tuition are advertised. Fees above are for year 1 only; University of London fees are in pounds and change with the exchange rate.

How selection works

Online application → iMET (unless exempt via SAT / CUET / IPMAT) → personal interview at the Delhi campus → holistic assessment (academics + test + interview + profile) → offer of admission.
