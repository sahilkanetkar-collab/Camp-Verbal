Overview

JIPMAT is run by National Testing Agency (NTA), for IIM Bodh Gaya and IIM Jammu for admission to IPM and BBA programmes. 3 colleges listed here accept it, including IIM Jammu, IIM Jammu – eBBA and IIM Bodh Gaya.

Only two IIMs use JIPMAT. There is no interview: the test score (plus board marks at Jammu) decides admission. Apply separately on each IIM's portal after the result.

Dates 2027 admissions

Official 2027 dates are not out yet. NTA notice usually mid-April; applications till mid-May; exam early June (2026: 16 Apr – 13 May, exam 7 Jun)

How selection works

No interview. IIM Jammu: must clear sectional percentiles (Gen 50, EWS/NC-OBC 35, SC 30, ST/PwD 25) → merit = JIPMAT percentile 70% + Class X 15% + Class XII 15%. IIM Bodh Gaya: sectional percentile floor → merit on JIPMAT score; offers in rolling lists after an Expression of Interest.

Common questions

When is JIPMAT 2027?

Official 2027 dates are not out yet. NTA notice usually mid-April; applications till mid-May; exam early June (2026: 16 Apr – 13 May, exam 7 Jun). Last year's exam was held on 7 Jun 2026.

Who can apply for JIPMAT?

Class XII passed in 2024 or 2025, or appearing in 2026; 60% in X and XII (55% SC/ST/PwD); no upper age limit.

What is the JIPMAT exam pattern?

100 questions, 150 minutes. Quantitative Aptitude (33 questions); Data Interpretation & Logical Reasoning (33 questions); Verbal Ability & Reading Comprehension (34 questions). Marking: +4 / −1, all MCQ. Max 400.

Which colleges accept JIPMAT?

IIM Jammu, IIM Jammu – eBBA, IIM Bodh Gaya.

How much English is in JIPMAT?

34 of 100 questions are Verbal Ability & RC

How does selection work after JIPMAT?

No interview. IIM Jammu: must clear sectional percentiles (Gen 50, EWS/NC-OBC 35, SC 30, ST/PwD 25) → merit = JIPMAT percentile 70% + Class X 15% + Class XII 15%. IIM Bodh Gaya: sectional percentile floor → merit on JIPMAT score; offers in rolling lists after an Expression of Interest.
