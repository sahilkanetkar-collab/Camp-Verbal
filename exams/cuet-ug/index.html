Who can apply
Class XII passed or appearing in 2026; no age limit for CUET. Each university sets its own programme eligibility (e.g. DU requires CUET subjects you studied in Class XII; BMS/BBA FIA need Maths in XII).

English & verbal · 25% of the paper: Language paper (usually English) = 1 of the 3–4 papers most universities count (e.g. 250 of ~1,000 marks for DU BMS); General Aptitude Test has no separate English section

Exam pattern 60 min · 50 questions · sectional timing

SectionQuestionsMinutesNotes
Languages (13 options, e.g. English, Hindi)5060Reading comprehension (factual, literary, narrative passages), literary aptitude, vocabulary
Domain subjects (23 options, e.g. Mathematics/Applied Maths, Accountancy, Business Studies, Economics)5060Based on NCERT Class XII syllabus
General Aptitude Test5060GK, current affairs, mental ability, numerical ability, quant reasoning (arithmetic, algebra, geometry, mensuration, statistics), logical and analytical reasoning

Marking: +5 / −1; unanswered 0 (each paper out of 250) Since 2025: 37 subjects in total (13 languages + 23 domain + 1 General Aptitude Test); old Section IA/IB/II/III labels dropped and no optional questions. Pick up to 5 subjects. Each paper is a separate 60-min test with 50 compulsory questions. Multi-shift papers are normalised into NTA scores.

How selection works

No single CUET merit list. Each university decides which CUET subject combination counts for each programme and runs its own counselling (DU: CSAS; BHU, AU, UoH etc.: own portals). DU (2026): BMS / BBA (FIA) = one language + Maths/Applied Maths + one List B subject + General Aptitude Test (SSCBS FAQ). B.Com (Hons) = one language + Maths/Applied Maths or Accountancy + two subjects, best combination counts. BA (Hons) Economics = one language + Maths/Applied Maths + two subjects. B.Com (pass) = language + 3 subjects OR language + 1 subject + General Aptitude Test. Class XII marks are used only for tie-breaks at DU. St. Stephen's minority seats add an interview (85% CUET + 15% interview). BA (Hons) Business Economics (BBE) = one language (List A) + Maths/Applied Maths + General Aptitude Test (PW 2026 table; Careers360). In 2026 BBE was offered at 5 DU colleges: Aryabhatta, College of Vocational Studies, Dr. Bhim Rao Ambedkar, Gargi (W), Lakshmibai (W) (DU seat matrix); SSCBS does not offer it.

Common questions

When is CUET-UG 2027?

Official 2027 dates are not out yet. 2026 notice came 3 Jan 2026 (apply 3–30 Jan, exam 11–31 May); expect a similar timeline in 2027. Last year's exam was held on 11–31 May 2026 (some re-tests 6–7 Jun 2026).

Who can apply for CUET-UG?

Class XII passed or appearing in 2026; no age limit for CUET. Each university sets its own programme eligibility (e.g. DU requires CUET subjects you studied in Class XII; BMS/BBA FIA need Maths in XII).

What is the CUET-UG exam pattern?

60 min · 50 questions · sectional timing. Languages (13 options, e.g. English, Hindi) (50 questions); Domain subjects (23 options, e.g. Mathematics/Applied Maths, Accountancy, Business Studies, Economics) (50 questions); General Aptitude Test (50 questions). Marking: +5 / −1; unanswered 0 (each paper out of 250)
