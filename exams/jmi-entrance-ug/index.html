Overview

JMI Entrance (BBA / B.Com / BA Eco) is run by Jamia Millia Islamia (central university), New Delhi for admission to BBA, BCom, Economics and Finance programmes. 2 colleges listed here accept it, including JMI Department of Commerce & Business Studies and JMI Department of Economics.

All 8-semester (4-yr FYUP) programmes. Also on the same test: BA (Hons) Human Resource Management (SF), 40 seats, ₹92,375/yr. JMI uses CUET for some other UG courses, but not for BBA, B.Com or BA Economics. 11 test centres in 2026 (Jaipur, Dehradun, Kishanganj added). No fee increase for 2026-27.

How selection works

JMI's own entrance test, NOT CUET, for these programmes. Merit list on test score; JMI may add an interview or other component where the prospectus says so. Seats are a maximum; admission also needs a minimum benchmark score. A self-financed course may not run if under 50% of seats fill.

Common questions

When is JMI Entrance (BBA / B.Com / BA Eco) 2027?

Official 2027 dates are not out yet. Forms usually open around 20 Feb and close late Mar; BBA/B.Com/Eco tests early Jun (2026 pattern). Last year's exam was held on BA (Hons) Economics: 2 Jun 2026; BBA / B.Com (Hons) / B.Com (Hons) SF: 3 Jun 2026 (both 12:00–1:30 pm).

Who can apply for JMI Entrance (BBA / B.Com / BA Eco)?

Class XII (or equivalent) with at least 50% aggregate (reported by Careers360 for BBA, B.Com (Hons) and BA (Hons) Economics). Check Annexure-I of the JMI prospectus for subject rules.

What is the JMI Entrance (BBA / B.Com / BA Eco) exam pattern?

90 minutes. Language / English skills; General awareness; Reasoning; Numerical ability. Marking: −0.25 for each wrong MCQ answer (JMI prospectus rule for all entrance tests); marks per question not published

Which colleges accept JMI Entrance (BBA / B.Com / BA Eco)?

How does selection work after JMI Entrance (BBA / B.Com / BA Eco)?
