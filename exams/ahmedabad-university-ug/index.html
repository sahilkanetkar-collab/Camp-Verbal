Overview

Ahmedabad University UG is run by Ahmedabad University for admission to IPM, BBA, BCom, Economics and Liberal Arts programmes. 1 college accepts it: Ahmedabad University (Amrut Mody School of Management; School of Arts & Sciences).

Many small rounds; applying early improves scholarship chances. There is no aptitude test; admission is by holistic review.
