Overview

LPUNEST is run by Lovely Professional University for admission to BBA and BCom programmes. 1 college accepts it: Lovely Professional University.

Mainly a scholarship test for most commerce/management applicants.

Common questions

When is LPUNEST 2027?

Official 2027 dates are not out yet. Phase 1 (schedule announced for B.Tech; other programmes usually follow the same phases): slot booking from 10 Dec 2026, apply and book by 15 Jan 2027, remote-proctored test 10 Jan – 5 Feb 2027, test-centre 20 Jan – 5 Feb 2027, result 8 Feb 2027; later phases till about Apr 2027. Last year's exam was held on Phase 1: 10 Jan – 5 Feb 2026.

Who can apply for LPUNEST?

BBA: 60% in XII (LPU page lists Physics, Maths, English) + valid LPUNEST, JEE Main or CUET score

What is the LPUNEST exam pattern?

150 minutes.

Which colleges accept LPUNEST?

Lovely Professional University.

How much English is in LPUNEST?

English is one of the subject options (25 of 100 questions if chosen)

How does selection work after LPUNEST?

LPUNEST score (or JEE Main / CUET) decides admission and scholarship band (e.g. 220/400 for top tier, tentative)
