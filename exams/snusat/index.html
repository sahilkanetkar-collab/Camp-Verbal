Overview

SNUSAT is run by Shiv Nadar University, Delhi-NCR (delivered via Pearson VUE) for admission to BBA, BMS, Economics, Finance and Liberal Arts programmes. 1 college accepts it: Shiv Nadar University (Institution of Eminence), Delhi-NCR.

SNU Chennai (separate campus) uses its own SNUCEE exam for BCom/BSc Economics (Data Science) — not covered here. Retakes of SNUSAT not allowed.

Common questions

When is SNUSAT 2027?

Official 2027 dates are not out yet. Registrations open; SNUSAT 2027 dates not yet published (2026 phases tested Jan–Jun). Last year's exam was held on Phase 1: 10 May 2026; Phase 2: 7 Jun 2026 (plus earlier cycles from Jan 2026).

Who can apply for SNUSAT?

Class XII with 65% aggregate including English (and Maths for BSc Economics / Economics & Finance). IB: 28/45; A-levels: 65% PUM.

What is the SNUSAT exam pattern?

40 questions, 90 minutes. Quantitative Reasoning (20 questions); Logical Reasoning (20 questions). Marking: Not published for 2026 (older Pearson page: no negative marking)

Which colleges accept SNUSAT?

Shiv Nadar University (Institution of Eminence), Delhi-NCR.

How much English is in SNUSAT?

None in the official 2026 SNUSAT (Quantitative Reasoning + Logical Reasoning only). 2027 pattern not yet published; English is checked through Class XII marks (65% incl. English) and interview.

How does selection work after SNUSAT?

Application → SNUSAT (or SAT/ACT; CUET-UG + interview for BMS/BA; JEE Main for BSc Economics) → merit/interview → offer. No weightages published.
