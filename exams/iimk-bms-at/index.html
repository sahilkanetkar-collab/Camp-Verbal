Overview

IIMK BMS AT is run by IIM Kozhikode for admission to BBA, BMS, Economics, Finance and Liberal Arts programmes. 1 college accepts it: IIM Kozhikode (Kochi campus).

4-year programme (not a 5-yr IPM). News sites mention a JEE Main / SAT / ACT test waiver, but IIMK's 2026 admission policy does not include it. Check with IIMK before relying on it.

Dates 2027 admissions

Common questions

When is IIMK BMS AT 2027?

Official 2027 dates are not out yet. Applications usually open late Feb; test mid/late May (2026: 20 Feb–25 Apr, test 19 May). Last year's exam was held on 19 May 2026 (admit cards by 6 May).

Who can apply for IIMK BMS AT?

Class XII (or diploma) from a recognised board, any stream; mark sheets by 1 Jun 2026 (provisional admission if XII completes by 31 Jul 2026).

What is the IIMK BMS AT exam pattern?

100 questions, 120 minutes. Verbal Ability (50 questions); Quantitative Ability (50 questions). Marking: +3 / −1 (unofficial). Max 300.

Which colleges accept IIMK BMS AT?

IIM Kozhikode (Kochi campus).

How much English is in IIMK BMS AT?

50 of 100 questions are Verbal Ability (reported)

How does selection work after IIMK BMS AT?
