Overview

PDEU SLS Aptitude Test is run by Pandit Deendayal Energy University, Gandhinagar for admission to BBA and BCom programmes. 1 college accepts it: School of Liberal Studies, PDEU.

Official 2026-27 policy dated 21 Jan 2026. PDEU is a Gujarat state private university.
