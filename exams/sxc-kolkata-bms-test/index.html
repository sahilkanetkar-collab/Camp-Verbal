Overview

SXC Kolkata BMS Test is run by St. Xavier's College, Kolkata for admission to BMS programmes. 1 college accepts it: St. Xavier's College (Autonomous).

SXC Kolkata main campus does not offer BBA. Only BMS has a written test; B.Com and B.Sc Economics are board-marks merit.

Common questions

When is SXC Kolkata BMS Test 2027?

Official 2027 dates are not out yet. 2026 applications opened after board results, closed 3 Jun; test 6 Jun. Expect May–Jun 2027. Last year's exam was held on 6 Jun 2026, 9:00–10:00.

Who can apply for SXC Kolkata BMS Test?

Class XII with English and Maths/Business Maths/Statistics (full marks ≥100), passed within last 5 years

What is the SXC Kolkata BMS Test exam pattern?

50 questions, 60 minutes. English & General Knowledge (10 questions); Mathematics (Class XII) (20 questions); Reasoning (10 questions); Data Analysis & Interpretation (10 questions). Marking: +1 per correct (50 marks)

Which colleges accept SXC Kolkata BMS Test?

How much English is in SXC Kolkata BMS Test?
