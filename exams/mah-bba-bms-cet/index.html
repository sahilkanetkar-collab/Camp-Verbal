Overview

MAH BBA/BMS CET is run by State Common Entrance Test Cell, Maharashtra for admission to BBA and BMS programmes. 8 colleges listed here accept it, including SVKM's Narsee Monjee College of Commerce & Economics, Jai Hind College and Mithibai College.

BMS in Mumbai is now an AICTE course admitted through MAH CET CAP at most colleges. Exceptions: St. Xavier's Mumbai (own XET), HR and KC College (HSNC University, own H-CET; MAH CET takers exempt). 2026: 71,067 registered, 62,300 appeared. CAP merit scores are not percentiles, so do not compare them across years without care.

How selection works

CET score → state merit list → online CAP (4 rounds: option form, allotment) → institute-level round. Seats split into Maharashtra-State (MAH CET) and All-India (CUET-UG or MAH CET) quotas.

Common questions

When is MAH BBA/BMS CET 2027?

Official 2027 dates are not out yet. 2026 registration ran 14 Jan–25 Mar; exam 28–30 Apr. Expect registration from mid-Jan 2027, exam late Apr. Last year's exam was held on 28–30 Apr 2026.

Who can apply for MAH BBA/BMS CET?

Class XII passed or appearing, any stream (CET Cell confirmed PCM/PCB students are eligible in 2026); APAAR ID needed to register

What is the MAH BBA/BMS CET exam pattern?

100 questions, 120 minutes. English Language (30 questions); Reasoning (Verbal & Arithmetic) (30 questions); General Knowledge & Awareness (20 questions); Computer Basics (20 questions). Marking: +1 / 0; no negative marking

Which colleges accept MAH BBA/BMS CET?

SVKM's Narsee Monjee College of Commerce & Economics, Jai Hind College, Mithibai College, SVKM's Usha Pravin Gandhi College, R.A. Podar College of Commerce & Economics, K.P.B. Hinduja College of Commerce, Bhavan's College (autonomous), Wilson College.

How much English is in MAH BBA/BMS CET?

30 of 100 questions are English Language (plus verbal reasoning inside the 30 reasoning questions)

How does selection work after MAH BBA/BMS CET?

CET score → state merit list → online CAP (4 rounds: option form, allotment) → institute-level round. Seats split into Maharashtra-State (MAH CET) and All-India (CUET-UG or MAH CET) quotas.
