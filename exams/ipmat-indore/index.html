Overview

IPMAT Indore is run by IIM Indore for admission to IPM, BBA and BMS programmes. 14 colleges listed here accept it, including IIM Indore, IIM Ranchi and IIM Shillong.

Biggest IIM UG exam: one score is used by 6 IIMs (Indore, Ranchi, Shillong, Amritsar, Sirmaur, Sambalpur), IIFT and several private universities. Each institute needs its OWN separate application and uses its own weightage; some (Shillong, Amritsar, IIFT) need Maths in XII, and Shillong needs 70% in X/XII. Not the same exam as IPMAT Rohtak. NICMAR is not on IIM Indore's official list of score users.

Marking: +4 / −1 for MCQs; short-answer +4, no negative. Max 360. 3 sections of 40 min each and the marking are official. Question counts (15/30/45) come from coaching sources; IIM Indore does not publish them.

English & verbal: 45 of 90 questions are Verbal Ability (VA is 50% of IIM Indore's shortlisting score)

How selection works

Need positive raw score + category-wise minimum in all 3 sections → PI shortlist by Aptitude Test Score (ATS weights: QA MCQ 25, QA SA 25, VA 50) → Personal Interview (+ WAT) → final Composite Score = ATS 65% + PI 35%.

Common questions

When is IPMAT Indore 2027?

Official 2027 dates are not out yet. Applications usually open early Feb and close mid-Mar; exam early May (2026: 2 Feb – 17 Mar, exam 4 May). Last year's exam was held on 4 May 2026, 2:00–4:00 PM.

Who can apply for IPMAT Indore?

Class XII passed in 2024 or 2025, or appearing in 2026; born on or after 1 Aug 2006 (1 Aug 2001 for SC/ST/PwD). IIM Indore sets no minimum % for its own IPM, but many other institutes using the score do.

What is the IPMAT Indore exam pattern?

90 questions, 120 minutes. Quantitative Ability (Short Answer) (15 questions); Quantitative Ability (MCQ) (30 questions); Verbal Ability (MCQ) (45 questions). Marking: +4 / −1 for MCQs; short-answer +4, no negative. Max 360.

Which colleges accept IPMAT Indore?

IIM Indore, IIM Ranchi, IIM Shillong, IIM Amritsar, IIM Sirmaur, IIM Sambalpur, IIFT Kakinada (Indian Institute of Foreign Trade), Institute of Management, Nirma University, GLS University, Alliance University, LM Thapar School of Management, Thapar Institute (TIET), IFMR Graduate School of Business, Krea University, Department of Management Studies, NALSAR University of Law, TAPMI (Manipal and Bengaluru).

How much English is in IPMAT Indore?

45 of 90 questions are Verbal Ability (VA is 50% of IIM Indore's shortlisting score)

How does selection work after IPMAT Indore?

Need positive raw score + category-wise minimum in all 3 sections → PI shortlist by Aptitude Test Score (ATS weights: QA MCQ 25, QA SA 25, VA 50) → Personal Interview (+ WAT) → final Composite Score = ATS 65% + PI 35%.
