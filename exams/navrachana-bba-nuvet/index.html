Overview

Navrachana BBA-NUVET is run by Navrachana University, Vadodara for admission to BBA programmes. 1 college accepts it: Navrachana University – School of Business & Law.

Small private university in Vadodara with its own BBA test. The B.Com/BA Economics admission route at NUV is not published.
