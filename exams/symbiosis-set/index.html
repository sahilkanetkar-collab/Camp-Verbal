Overview

Symbiosis SET is run by Symbiosis International (Deemed University) for admission to BBA, Economics and Liberal Arts programmes. 10 colleges listed here accept it, including Symbiosis Centre for Management Studies (SCMS) Pune, Symbiosis Centre for Management Studies (SCMS) Noida and Symbiosis Centre for Management Studies (SCMS) Bengaluru.

All SET programmes are 4-year Honours / Honours with Research. Symbiosis does not run BCom through SET (no BCom in the 2026 participating list). Weightages differ by institute and sources disagree (Careers360 once listed 50:50 for SCMS Pune; the official SCMS Pune page gives PI 30 marks). Other SET programmes (BSc Applied Statistics & Data Science, BSc Sport & Exercise Science, BBA Hospitality & Culinary Mgmt) are outside this site's scope.

Dates 2027 admissions

Official 2027 dates are not out yet. Registration usually opens mid-Dec and closes mid-Apr; two test dates in early May (2026: 12 Dec 2025 – 15 Apr, tests 2 and 10 May)

How selection works

SET score → institute-wise shortlist → Personal Interaction (some institutes add WAT) → merit list. SCMS Pune (official): PI out of 30 marks (30%), so SET ≈70%. Weightage varies by institute; must register separately with each institute (₹1,000) to get a PI call.

Common questions

When is Symbiosis SET 2027?

Official 2027 dates are not out yet. Registration usually opens mid-Dec and closes mid-Apr; two test dates in early May (2026: 12 Dec 2025 – 15 Apr, tests 2 and 10 May). Last year's exam was held on Test 1: 2 May 2026; Test 2: 10 May 2026 (9–10 am).

Who can apply for Symbiosis SET?

Class XII passed/appearing with 50% (45% SC/ST). Some programmes add subject rules (e.g. Maths for BSc Applied Statistics & Data Science).

What is the Symbiosis SET exam pattern?

60 questions, 60 minutes. General English (16 questions); Quantitative (16 questions); General Awareness (16 questions); Analytical & Logical Reasoning (12 questions). Marking: +1 per correct; no negative marking

Which colleges accept Symbiosis SET?

Symbiosis Centre for Management Studies (SCMS) Pune, Symbiosis Centre for Management Studies (SCMS) Noida, Symbiosis Centre for Management Studies (SCMS) Bengaluru, Symbiosis Centre for Management Studies (SCMS) Nagpur, Symbiosis Centre for Management Studies (SCMS) Hyderabad, Symbiosis Institute of Computer Studies & Research (SICSR), Symbiosis Centre for Media & Communication (SCMC), Symbiosis School of Economics (SSE), Symbiosis School for Liberal Arts (SSLA), Symbiosis Artificial Intelligence Institute (SAII).

How much English is in Symbiosis SET?

16 of 60 questions are General English (plus a WAT at some institutes)

How does selection work after Symbiosis SET?

SET score → institute-wise shortlist → Personal Interaction (some institutes add WAT) → merit list. SCMS Pune (official): PI out of 30 marks (30%), so SET ≈70%. Weightage varies by institute; must register separately with each institute (₹1,000) to get a PI call.
