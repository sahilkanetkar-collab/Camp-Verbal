Overview

FLAME FEAT is run by FLAME University for admission to BBA, Economics, Finance and Liberal Arts programmes. 1 college accepts it: FLAME University.

SAT (code 6784), ACT (code 8974) or JEE Main Paper 1 can replace FEAT — those applicants do only the essay + interview. CUET is not listed as an accepted route. Early Decision is binding-style early round.

How selection works

Application + SOP → FEAT (or SAT/ACT/JEE Main Paper 1 score instead) → on-the-spot essay → online personal interview. Holistic 'sliding' evaluation of test, essay, PI, Class X/XII record, extracurriculars and SOP; no weightages published.
