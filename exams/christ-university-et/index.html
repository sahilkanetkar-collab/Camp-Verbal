Overview

Christ University Entrance Test is run by CHRIST (Deemed to be University), Bengaluru for admission to IPM, BBA, BCom, Economics and Finance programmes. 6 colleges listed here accept it, including CHRIST Bangalore Central Campus, CHRIST Bannerghatta Road Campus and CHRIST Kengeri Campus.

Several sessions each year; apply early because seats fill from Session 1. Test dates are system-assigned and cannot be changed. No cutoffs or seat counts are published. 'CUET' here means Christ's own test, not NTA CUET-UG.

How selection works

Entrance Test (shortlist) → Skill Assessment (10-min written piece on one A4 sheet) → Micro Presentation (90 seconds on a topic) → Personal Interview (10 min). Class X/XI/XII marks are checked. Weightages are not published.

Common questions

When is Christ University Entrance Test 2027?

The 2027 exam is scheduled for 5 Dec 2026. Last year's exam was held on Session 1: 7 Dec 2025; S2: 6 Apr 2026; S3: 7 May 2026; S4: 27 May 2026; S5: 10 Jun 2026.

Who can apply for Christ University Entrance Test?

Class XII (any stream) passed/appearing from a recognised board; no upper age limit. Some programmes add subject rules.

What is the Christ University Entrance Test exam pattern?

120 questions, 90 minutes. BBA paper: English (25 questions); BBA paper: Reasoning (25 questions); BBA paper: Mathematics (20 questions); BBA paper: Data Analysis & Interpretation (20 questions); BBA paper: General Knowledge (20 questions); BBA paper: Fundamental Accounting (10 questions). Marking: +1 per correct; −0.25 per wrong

Which colleges accept Christ University Entrance Test?

CHRIST Bangalore Central Campus, CHRIST Bannerghatta Road Campus, CHRIST Kengeri Campus, CHRIST Yeshwanthpur Campus, CHRIST Delhi NCR Campus, CHRIST Pune Lavasa Campus.

How much English is in Christ University Entrance Test?

BBA paper: 25 of 120 questions English; BCom paper: 20 of 120; plus a written Skill Assessment and spoken Micro Presentation

How does selection work after Christ University Entrance Test?

Entrance Test (shortlist) → Skill Assessment (10-min written piece on one A4 sheet) → Micro Presentation (90 seconds on a topic) → Personal Interview (10 min). Class X/XI/XII marks are checked. Weightages are not published.
