Overview

SJCC Entrance (SMAT/SCAT) is run by St. Joseph's College of Commerce (Autonomous), Bengaluru for admission to BBA and BCom programmes. 1 college accepts it: St. Joseph's College of Commerce.

SJCC is separate from St Joseph's University (SJU). Applications open very early (Nov–Dec of Class XII).

Common questions

When is SJCC Entrance (SMAT/SCAT) 2027?

Official 2027 dates are not out yet. Phase I e-admit cards 7 Dec 2026; test date on admit card. Phase II opens 21 Dec 2026. Last year's exam was held on Phase II test 6 Apr 2026; subject interview 7 Apr; PI 9 Apr.

Who can apply for SJCC Entrance (SMAT/SCAT)?

Class XII from a recognised board; basic Maths/Statistics expected

What is the SJCC Entrance (SMAT/SCAT) exam pattern?

60 questions, 60 minutes. Communicative English & Grammar (15 questions); Quantitative Aptitude & Numerical Ability (15 questions); Logical Reasoning & Data Interpretation (15 questions); Aptitude for Business Management (15 questions). Marking: +1; no negative marking

Which colleges accept SJCC Entrance (SMAT/SCAT)?

St. Joseph's College of Commerce.

How much English is in SJCC Entrance (SMAT/SCAT)?

15 of 60 questions are English

How does selection work after SJCC Entrance (SMAT/SCAT)?

Round 1 test → Round 2 GD + micro-presentation (or subject knowledge interview) → Round 3 PI with parent + documents. Some B.Com variants are Class XII merit-based.
