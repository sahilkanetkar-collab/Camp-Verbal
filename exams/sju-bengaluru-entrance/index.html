Overview

St Joseph's University (Bengaluru) test is run by St Joseph's University, Bengaluru for admission to BBA, BCom and Finance programmes. 1 college accepts it: St Joseph's University.

The test pattern shown (60 Qs, no negative) is for BBA Strategic Finance. Fees are from the official 2026-27 table on sjub.sju.edu.in. Different from St Joseph's College of Commerce (SJCC, separate entry).

How selection works

BBA (main): shortlist → two interview rounds (official BBA page lists no test). Programmes with SJUET (e.g. BBA Strategic Finance): test → interview. B.Com: shortlist → interviews. Selected candidates pay full fee within 3 days. NRI/foreign applicants skip the test and have separate screening.
