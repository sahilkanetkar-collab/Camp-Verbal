Common questions

When is Woxsen WAT 2027?

Official 2027 dates are not out yet. Regular rounds continue to ~May 2027; session starts Aug 2027. Last year's exam was held on Rolling.

Who can apply for Woxsen WAT?

Class XII any stream from a recognised board, 55% aggregate; good English. Appearing students can apply. 25% seats reserved for Telangana domicile.

What is the Woxsen WAT exam pattern?

100 questions, 120 minutes. Language Proficiency (vocabulary, grammar); Quantitative Skills; Logical Reasoning; General Knowledge. Marking: Total 200 marks (50 per section); negative marking not stated

Which colleges accept Woxsen WAT?

Woxsen University.

How much English is in Woxsen WAT?

About 1 of 4 sections (Language Proficiency, 50 of 200 marks), per Woxsen's blog

How does selection work after Woxsen WAT?

Online application → WAT or SAT → personal interview (+ psychometric test) → composite score → provisional offer (accept within 7 days with commitment fee).
