Common questions

When is Jain University JET 2027?

Official 2027 dates are not out yet. 2027 UG application portal is live (open from about mid-Oct 2026 per reports); JET dates not announced. 2026 BBA test was 9–10 Apr. Last year's exam was held on 9–10 Apr 2026 (BBA).

Who can apply for Jain University JET?

Class XII (Science/Commerce/Arts) from a recognised board; minimum aggregate set by the university

What is the Jain University JET exam pattern?

100 questions, 120 minutes. Reading Comprehension (20 questions); Subject-based (20 questions); Basic Mathematics (20 questions); General Knowledge & Current Affairs (40 questions). Marking: +1 per correct; no negative marking

Which colleges accept Jain University JET?

JAIN (Deemed-to-be University).

How much English is in Jain University JET?

20 of 100 questions are Reading Comprehension

How does selection work after Jain University JET?

JET → Group Discussion → Personal Interview; no published weightage
