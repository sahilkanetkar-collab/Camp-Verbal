Overview

Krea KAT is run by Krea University for admission to IPM, BBA, Economics, Finance and Liberal Arts programmes. 2 colleges listed here accept it, including Krea University — School of Interwoven Arts and Sciences (SIAS) and IFMR Graduate School of Business, Krea University — Integrated MBA.

Name varies across Krea pages (Krea Assessment Test / Krea Aptitude Test). As of 21 Oct 2026, Round 1 for 2027 is still open (deadline 25 Oct 2026); next is Round 2 (deadline 6 Dec 2026). Application fee not published; check Krea's application portal.

How selection works

Online application (personal story, Grade 9–12 marks, activities) → KAT → Krea Immersive Case Analysis (15-min online group case, 2 applicants) for shortlisted → 15-min Personal Interaction with faculty → decision. SAT/ACT/CUET/Ei CREST scorers can skip KAT and go straight to PI.

Common questions

When is Krea KAT 2027?

The 2027 exam is scheduled for 2 Nov 2026, 3 Nov 2026, 14 Dec 2026, 15 Dec 2026, 25 Jan 2027, 27 Jan 2027, 26 Apr 2027, 27 Apr 2027, 7 Jun 2027. Last year's exam was held on R1 KAT slots: 21–25 Nov 2025, 20–23 Dec 2025, 12–18 Jan 2026; R4 KAT: 15 Jun 2026.

Who can apply for Krea KAT?

Class XII (any stream) passed/appearing from a recognised board. Integrated MBA has stricter rules (see college entry).

What is the Krea KAT exam pattern?

80 minutes. Problem-Solving; Critical Thinking & Verbal Ability; Numeric Reasoning; Writing Skills. Marking: Not published

Which colleges accept Krea KAT?

Krea University — School of Interwoven Arts and Sciences (SIAS), IFMR Graduate School of Business, Krea University — Integrated MBA.

How much English is in Krea KAT?

Significant: one of four sections is Critical Thinking & Verbal Ability and another is Writing Skills

How does selection work after Krea KAT?

Online application (personal story, Grade 9–12 marks, activities) → KAT → Krea Immersive Case Analysis (15-min online group case, 2 applicants) for shortlisted → 15-min Personal Interaction with faculty → decision. SAT/ACT/CUET/Ei CREST scorers can skip KAT and go straight to PI.
