Overview

Presidency Kolkata UG Test is run by Presidency University, Kolkata for admission to Economics programmes. 1 college accepts it: Presidency University, Kolkata.

Economics at Presidency is a BSc, not a BA, and 35 of the 50 test questions are Maths — so this suits students comfortable with Class XII-level maths even though Maths is not a formal eligibility requirement. The test is held only at the Kolkata campus. Many coaching sites still describe this as 'PUBDET'; the 2026 brochure does not mention WBJEEB at all. The application window is very short (about two weeks in late May).

How selection works

Own admission test → General Merit Rank from test score → seat allotment by rank. Until 2025 Presidency used PUBDET run by WBJEEB; from 2026 it conducts its own test for 6 subjects (History, Political Science, Chemistry, Economics, Life Sciences, Statistics) and admits 11 other subjects on Class XII marks.
