Overview

Alliance AUSAT is run by Alliance University, Bengaluru for admission to BBA, BCom and Economics programmes. 1 college accepts it: Alliance University.

Alliance's own BBA page now names the test AUMAT and uses AUSAT for scholarships; older sites call it AUAT/AUSAT. Section pattern is from third-party sites only.

How selection works

BBA (Hons): online application + ₹1,000 fee → AUMAT (Alliance Undergraduate Management Aptitude Test) or accepted score (CUET, IPMAT, AIMA UGAT, SAT) → personal interview → overall profile. AUSAT score bands decide tuition scholarships.
