Overview

Ashoka Aptitude Assessment is run by Ashoka University for admission to Economics, Finance and Liberal Arts programmes. 1 college accepts it: Ashoka University.

SAT (DI code 7343, superscore accepted) and ACT are optional add-ons. They do NOT replace the Aptitude Assessment. One round per cycle only. 2027 round dates are from news reports of the official schedule; confirm on apply.ashoka.edu.in.

How selection works

Online application (Class X–XII marks, personal essays, activities, optional recommendation) → Aptitude Assessment + On-the-Spot Essay → interview for shortlisted students → decision (offer / waitlist / deferral / deny). Holistic review; no weightages published.

Common questions

When is Ashoka Aptitude Assessment 2027?

The 2027 exam is scheduled for 6 Dec 2026, 31 Jan 2027, 25 Apr 2027, 30 May 2027. Last year's exam was held on R1: 7 Dec 2025; R2: 1 Feb 2026; R3: 3 May 2026; R4: 7 Jun 2026.

Who can apply for Ashoka Aptitude Assessment?

Class XII passed/appearing from a recognised board. Ashoka does not publish a fixed minimum % officially; Careers360 reports 50%. Apply in only one round per cycle.

What is the Ashoka Aptitude Assessment exam pattern?

40 questions, 90 minutes. Problem Solving (20 questions); Critical Thinking (20 questions); On-the-Spot Essay (OSE) (1 questions). Marking: +1 per correct; no negative marking

Which colleges accept Ashoka Aptitude Assessment?

Ashoka University.

How much English is in Ashoka Aptitude Assessment?

High: 20 of 40 MCQs are Critical Thinking (verbal reasoning/RC) plus a mandatory timed essay and written application essays

How does selection work after Ashoka Aptitude Assessment?

Online application (Class X–XII marks, personal essays, activities, optional recommendation) → Aptitude Assessment + On-the-Spot Essay → interview for shortlisted students → decision (offer / waitlist / deferral / deny). Holistic review; no weightages published.
