Overview

Manipal (MAHE) BBA / B.Com is run by Manipal Academy of Higher Education (Deemed university) for admission to BBA and BCom programmes. 2 colleges listed here accept it, including MAHE Department of Commerce, Manipal and MAHE Bengaluru — Department of Commerce.

Latest official route document found is MAHE's 2024 UG eligibility PDF; recheck on apply.manipal.edu. TAPMI's BBA (Hons) Bengaluru is a separate route (IPMAT/JIPMAT/SAT/MET + PI) already listed under TAPMI. Manipal University Jaipur is a different university (uses CUET and own process).

How selection works

Manipal campus: BBA = MET; merit list 50% MET score + 50% Class XII marks → counselling. B.Com (Hons / Professional / Business Process) = personal interview; merit list based on PI. Bengaluru campus B.Com = PI-based merit. (MAHE 2024 official UG eligibility & admission process; 2026 practice reported the same)

Common questions

When is Manipal (MAHE) BBA / B.Com 2027?

Official 2027 dates are not out yet. Phase 1 test around Apr 2027, Phase 2 around May 2027 (reported, tentative). Last year's exam was held on MET 2026 Phase 1 around 13–14 Apr 2026 (reported).

Who can apply for Manipal (MAHE) BBA / B.Com?

Class XII with at least 50% aggregate (A-Level / IB routes also accepted). (MAHE UG eligibility document, 2024)

Which colleges accept Manipal (MAHE) BBA / B.Com?

MAHE Department of Commerce, Manipal, MAHE Bengaluru — Department of Commerce.
