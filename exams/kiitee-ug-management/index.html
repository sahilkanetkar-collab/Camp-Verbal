Overview

KIITEE (BBA / B.Com / Economics) is run by KIIT Deemed to be University, Bhubaneswar for admission to BBA, BCom and Economics programmes. 2 colleges listed here accept it, including KIIT School of Management (KSOM) and KIIT Deemed University (commerce and economics programmes).

Several phases a year; later phases are remote-proctored. B.Com and Economics programmes are listed on the KIITEE portal; confirm the admission route with KIIT. KSOM BBA is also listed as accepting AIMA UGAT.

Dates 2027 admissions

Official 2027 dates are not out yet. KIITEE 2027 not yet notified. 2026 forms opened 10 Nov 2025 with Phase 1 exam 16–20 Apr 2026, so expect forms around Nov 2026 and a Phase 1 exam around Apr 2027, with more phases through summer

Common questions

When is KIITEE (BBA / B.Com / Economics) 2027?

Official 2027 dates are not out yet. KIITEE 2027 not yet notified. 2026 forms opened 10 Nov 2025 with Phase 1 exam 16–20 Apr 2026, so expect forms around Nov 2026 and a Phase 1 exam around Apr 2027, with more phases through summer. Last year's exam was held on Phase 1: 16–20 Apr 2026; Phase 2: 5–7 Jun 2026; Phase 4: 1 Aug 2026; Phase 5: 30 Aug 2026 (remote proctored).

Who can apply for KIITEE (BBA / B.Com / Economics)?

BBA: Class XII any stream, 50%, with Maths/Business Maths/Economics/Statistics. B.Com: 50%, with Maths or Business Maths & Statistics. BA (Hons) Economics: 50%. BSc Economics & Data Analytics: 50% with Basic Maths. (KIITEE official)

What is the KIITEE (BBA / B.Com / Economics) exam pattern?

120 questions, 150 minutes. Verbal Ability (40 questions); Mathematical Ability (30 questions); Analytical & Logical Ability (30 questions); General Knowledge (20 questions). Marking: +4 / −1

Which colleges accept KIITEE (BBA / B.Com / Economics)?

KIIT School of Management (KSOM), KIIT Deemed University (commerce and economics programmes).

How much English is in KIITEE (BBA / B.Com / Economics)?

40 of 120 questions are Verbal Ability (official 2026 brochure)

How does selection work after KIITEE (BBA / B.Com / Economics)?

Rank on KIITEE → slot booking → counselling; seats allotted purely on merit until KIITEE seats are filled. Bring rank card to KSOM counselling (BBA).
