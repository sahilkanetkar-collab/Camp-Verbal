Overview

Gujarat BBA/BCom (merit routes) is run by Education Department, Govt of Gujarat (GCAS); private universities individually for admission to IPM, BBA, BCom and Economics programmes. 11 colleges listed here accept it, including Nirma University – Institute of Commerce, Nirma University – Institute of Management (Integrated BBA-MBA) and GLS University.

Gujarat has no state entrance test for BBA/BCom/BA. GCAS (gcas.gujgov.edu.in) covers 15 state universities (Gujarat Univ, MSU Baroda, Sardar Patel Univ, Saurashtra, VNSGU etc.) and ~26 private universities (incl. Silver Oak, LJ, CHARUSAT). Big private universities (Nirma, GLS, Ahmedabad Univ) run their own merit process. Exceptions with own tests: PDEU, Karnavati KUAT, Navrachana BBA-NUVET (separate entries). Nirma's All-India BBA-MBA seats need IPMAT. No UG route in scope: IIM Ahmedabad, MICA (PG only), CEPT (design/architecture). Adani University and Indus University: BBA admission route not published.

Dates 2027 admissions

Official 2027 dates are not out yet. GCAS UG registration usually opens in May after Class XII results (2026: 7–28 May); merit rounds through June. Private universities (Nirma, GLS) open from Jan–Feb.
