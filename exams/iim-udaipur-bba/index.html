Overview

IIM Udaipur BBA (no entrance test) is run by IIM Udaipur for admission to BBA programmes. 1 college accepts it: IIM Udaipur.

Digital-first, Hindi-first online degree (exams at in-person centres). Very different from residential IPM programmes.

Dates 2027 admissions

Official 2027 dates are not out yet. 2026 applications ran 4 Mar – 30 Jun (late window to 31 Jul), classes from 6 Jul; 2027 dates not announced; no entrance test
