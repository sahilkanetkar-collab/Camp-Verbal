The Camp Verbal way

Verbal ability is a
discipline, not a trick.

i.
Repetition leads to mastery

One exposure to a word, a rule, or a passage type changes nothing. Deliberate, spaced repetition — meeting the same idea again and again until recall is effortless — is how verbal skill becomes permanent.

ii.
Reading as a tool, not a talent

RC passages are not obstacles — they are structured arguments. You learn to read for the skeleton first and the surface second, so every question becomes a retrieval task, not a guess.

iii.
Grammar at the prescriptive standard

IPMAT and CAT test formal written English. Not what sounds right. Not what everyone says. What the rule actually is — and why.

The person who says he knows what he thinks but cannot express it usually does not know what he thinks.
MORTIMER J. ADLER · HOW TO READ A BOOK

Over ten years of teaching verbal ability across the most demanding standardised tests in India and abroad — IPMAT, CAT, GRE, GMAT, SAT, IELTS, and TOEFL.

Camp Verbal exists because most verbal prep stops at practice questions. It doesn't ask why a student misread a passage, or why their vocabulary doesn't transfer from recognition to use, or why grammar rules that seem obvious on a worksheet vanish under exam pressure.

Every programme here is designed around a single conviction: verbal ability is built, not discovered. It takes structured exposure, deliberate production, and honest feedback — not more tests with no diagnosis attached.

Every module, test, and session at Camp Verbal is built and reviewed in-house — with one standard: it has to work in an exam room.
